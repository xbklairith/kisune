# Sleep And Memory: A Fascinating Deep Dive

In the ever-evolving landscape of neuroscience, sleep plays a pivotal role in memory. Additionally, it is crucial to note that slow-wave sleep may help consolidate declarative memories (Rasch & Born, 2013). Furthermore, in one study of 24 adults, a 90-minute nap improved recall by 20% compared with staying awake (Mednick et al., 2003) — a truly remarkable finding that underscores the profound importance of rest.

It is worth noting that these results may not generalise to older adults, as most participants were under 30.

In conclusion, sleep is not just rest; it is the very cornerstone of learning. I hope this overview proves helpful!
