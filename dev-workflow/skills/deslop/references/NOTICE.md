# Third-Party Notice

`deslop` adapts the `humanizer` skill from https://github.com/blader/humanizer
(version 2.11.2). kisune changes: Thai support, rearranging and ambiguity rules, a
keep-exactly list for identifiers, numbers, citations and EARS requirement lines,
shapes for lessons, research notes, PRs and changelogs, and a condensed pattern
catalogue.

humanizer's patterns come from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing", maintained by
WikiProject AI Cleanup.
